# TurnipZ

A modified Mesa Turnip Vulkan driver focused on performance, stability, and compatibility improvements for emulation on Adreno GPUs.

Use at your own risk.

## FAQ

### Recommended setup
Tested on Eden v0.2.0-rc2 and Eden Nightly with an overclocked Adreno 650 GPU (905 MHz). Results may vary depending on the device, emulator, and game.

### Device compatibility
Primarily developed and tested for Adreno 650. Other Adreno GPUs may work but remain untested.

### Emulator compatibility
Officially tested on Eden. Compatibility with other emulators and applications is not guaranteed.
